Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Silvra works the moment you open it.

Where are my photos stored?

On your device, inside the app. We do not upload your photos or edits anywhere. Your originals are kept as they are, and edits are saved separately.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Editing, the library and export all work without a connection. Only buying or restoring Pro needs the internet.

How does Pro billing work?

Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in your Apple ID settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends. There is also a one-time purchase that never renews.

Can I move my looks to another phone?

Yes. Save a look as a recipe and share its code. Paste the code on another device to get the same look.
