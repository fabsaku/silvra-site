Support

Write to support@morkrum.app and tell us what you were doing, which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened. A screenshot helps. We usually answer within two working days.

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the library, tap Upgrade, then tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID that you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Mörkrum and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Where are my photos?

Your originals stay in your normal Photos library. Mörkrum keeps its own copy of the photos you import, plus your edits, inside the app. Deleting a photo from the Mörkrum library does not delete it from Photos.

My exported photo is not in Photos.

Tap Save to Photos in the export sheet and allow adding photos when iOS asks. If you tapped Don't Allow, open Settings, Mörkrum, Photos and choose Add Photos Only.

I deleted the app. Are my edits gone?

Yes. Edits and the app's copies of your photos are stored inside the app, so they are removed with it. Your originals in Photos are not affected.

How do I move a look to another phone?

Save the look as a recipe on the Presets tab, tap Copy code, send the code to yourself, and paste it under Import code on the other phone. Recipes need Pro.
