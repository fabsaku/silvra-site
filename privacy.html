Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Silvra ("the app", "we") is made by Fabian SK, Stockholm, Sweden. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your photos stay on your phone, and we collect almost nothing.

Your photos and edits

Photos you import, the edits you make and the looks you save are stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not upload them, copy them to a server or look at them. If you delete the app or a photo from the library, that data is deleted with it.

Access to your photo library

When you import, the app uses the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose. When you export, the app asks for permission to add photos to your photo library. It does not read the rest of your library.

What we do not collect

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts, and the app does not contain third-party advertising or analytics software.

Purchases

Silvra Pro is bought through the App Store, and Apple handles payment. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to check whether a purchase is active. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier generated for the app installation, and it uses this to tell the app whether Pro is unlocked. It does not receive your photos or your name. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's handling of your payment information is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. If you sign up for launch news, your email app sends your address to us, and we keep it only to send you that message, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Contacting us for support

If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it for as long as needed for that purpose.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal data, there is little to access or delete. If you have emailed us or signed up for news and want your information removed, write to us and we will delete it. If you are in the EU or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for material changes we will tell you in the app.
